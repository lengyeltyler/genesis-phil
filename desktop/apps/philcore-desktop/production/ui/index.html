Recovery & backup

Your validator and separate recovery authority are encrypted under Mac-protected storage. Your portable backup preserves the same identity and account, encrypted with your passphrase. Keep it somewhere you control, separately from the passphrase. Phil keeps no recovery copy. Losing both protected Mac storage and the backup may mean losing access. A stolen backup plus its passphrase can restore your authority.

The recovery role can replace a lost validator after the contract delay. It is separate from ordinary mint/transfer authorization. No cloud custodian or additional wallet account is created.

Platform enrollment and a verified portable backup are required before custody is ready. Restoring preserves the same account and creates a new local Mac binding. Collection deployment and current network state are checked separately before funding.
