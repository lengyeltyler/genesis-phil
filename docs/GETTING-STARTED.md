# Install and mint with Phil

[Back to download](../README.md)

Read the requirements and current preview status on the download page first. This guide applies to Build 87 on Apple Silicon Macs.

1. **Download Phil.** Use the [official download](https://github.com/lengyeltyler/genesis-phil/releases/download/v0.3.0-build87/Phil-0.3.0-87-macOS-arm64.dmg). The matching checksum is on the [verification page](VERIFY-DOWNLOAD.md).
2. **Install.** Open the disk image and drag Phil to Applications. Do not bypass Gatekeeper or remove quarantine.
3. **Open Phil.** Default providers are configured automatically: dRPC and independent PublicNode reads, with the public Candide bundler. You do not need a developer account. These public services have rate limits and can be unavailable. Optional **Advanced provider settings** accepts your own Alchemy Mainnet/bundler and Infura Mainnet keys; those plans can have quotas and charges.
4. **Create identity.** Enter a name and a passphrase containing at least 12 characters, uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol. Confirm it and choose **Create Phil Identity**. The Desktop policy permits only Genesis mint and transfer with protected Mac confirmation. Phone-required identities cannot substitute this policy for phone approval.
5. **Unlock and back up.** Unlock Phil with your passphrase. In **Recovery & backup**, enter the passphrase and choose **Save encrypted backup with Mac confirmation**. Choose a new filename, approve the native Mac prompt, and store the file offline and the passphrase separately. Enter the passphrase again and choose **Open and verify saved backup**. Funding is blocked until verification succeeds. The backup contains both authority roles under the same passphrase; this is not independent-device or independent-secret recovery. Neither a lost passphrase nor loss of both copies can be repaired by Phil.
6. **Prepare your Phil account.** Unlocking prepares one account address for this identity. Open **Account & funding** to view it. The account is created on Ethereum together with your first mint, so preparing the address costs nothing. On a fresh installation, **Restore an existing Phil Identity** restores the same account from a backup for this exact collection/configuration and its passphrase and binds it to this Mac. Restart and unlock. A successful restore already verifies the backup; no second backup or repeated verification is required. Check current network readiness before funding. Never replace existing identity storage to force a restore.
7. **Check fees, then fund.** Choose **Refresh fees and funding estimate**. Mint price is **0 ETH**; Ethereum network fees are separate. The screen shows current base fee, bundler recommendations, account setup and mint estimates, required prefund, safety margin, recommended balance, current balance, shortfall and refresh time. Estimates expire after 30 seconds. If collection deployment is pending, stop and do not fund. Once the app verifies the deployed contracts, copy the account address and send the displayed shortfall in ETH on **Ethereum Mainnet only**, using another wallet. Spare ETH has no withdrawal action under this Genesis policy; avoid excess funding.
8. **Wait for funding readiness.** Refresh after Ethereum confirms your funding. The app checks balances using both providers. Refresh again if the quote expires or fees change. A fee estimate is a conservative budget, not a guaranteed final charge.
9. **Re-roll.** In **Discover**, click **Reroll** as often as you like. Rerolling is local and free. **Traits** identifies the selected layers; **Expand** enlarges the view. A preview does not reserve a recipe. Official reroll probabilities do not guarantee the final collection's distribution.
10. **Pick your Phil.** Choose **Keep this Phil**. The current and previous roll are saved locally for this account and configuration, before a new roll is displayed. Locking or reopening restores your choice. **Continue browsing** requires confirmation; **Undo last reroll** restores and keeps the previous choice. These preferences are not included in the encrypted identity backup and are not an on-chain reservation.
11. **Mint this Phil.** Review the exact artwork and generated name, Ethereum Mainnet, account, collection, recipient and maximum Ethereum network fee. The mint price remains 0 ETH. If the recipe is taken or supply is exhausted, Phil stops rather than replacing your selection. A review expires when its fee quote expires. Starting a review automatically keeps the selection. A failed review never rerolls it.
12. **Confirm on your Mac.** Click **Authorize with protected Mac confirmation** and approve the native macOS dialog. Cancel to deny. This authorizes the reviewed operation only. There is no Desktop fallback for a phone-required identity; that native phone path is unavailable in this release.
13. **Wait for Ethereum.** Progress distinguishes Mac approval, signing, submission and waiting for confirmation. Do not resubmit after a timeout. Phil records the attempt before submission and never automatically retries a signed operation. Use **My minted Phil → Check pending submission** if needed. Pending is not confirmation; a reverted operation can still cost gas.
14. **View My minted Phil.** Ownership refreshes after a confirmed mint or transfer. You can also choose **Refresh ownership**. Phil verifies ownership and the confirmed operation through both providers. The token receives its sequential number when minted, producing a title such as Philstradomous #1. Names are tied to the artwork recipe and can repeat; numbered titles identify individual tokens.
15. **Transfer if desired.** In **Transfer Phil**, select the owned Phil and enter a nonzero Ethereum recipient address. Choose **Review transfer**, verify the recipient and fee, then approve a fresh Mac confirmation. A receiving contract must accept ERC-721 transfers. Refresh ownership afterward. Transferring does not reset your account's lifetime mint eligibility.
16. **Optional donation.** Clicking **tylerlengyel.eth** copies the ENS name. Donate separately from a suitable wallet if you wish. Donations are not mint fees; the Genesis account exposes no general ETH-send action.

If fees expire, close the review and refresh. If providers disagree or are unavailable, wait and refresh manually; independent verification is never dropped. If a submission is ambiguous, reconcile the recorded attempt instead of sending again. Invalid recipients, unavailable recipes, sold-out supply and insufficient ETH stop the operation. A cancelled Mac prompt must not sign or submit anything.

Phil Genesis is an experimental Mainnet application. Protected authorization does not guarantee asset value or safety against every compromised Mac. The on-chain authority is ECDSA. The NFT is fully on-chain, transferable and immutable, with a 369 supply cap, one lifetime mint per account and 3.69% ERC-2981 royalty information; marketplace payment is not guaranteed. Keep backups and your passphrase safe and avoid storing unnecessary ETH in the restricted account.

Choose a new filename when saving a backup. Phil preserves existing backups and does not overwrite them. After choosing the destination, approve Mac confirmation. Re-enter the original identity passphrase when opening and verifying the saved backup. If verification fails, keep both your vault and backup; the error identifies the validation stage without showing secrets.

No Terminal, Xcode, Node, Git, manual JSON editing or developer account is part of normal use. This tutorial describes Build 87; the published release record separately records actual signed-artifact execution evidence. It is not a launch announcement.

## Launch access and reserved mints

During the first 12 hours after the bootstrap deploys, only a Genesis account registered by one of the three approved early wallets may mint. Copy your Genesis account address from the app and complete the separate owner-controlled registration before reviewing a mint. Preserve the normal backup and protected-authorization steps. Other users may mint once public access opens, until the 336 general slots are used. The 33 reserved slots require an individual deployer grant to the recipient's Genesis account after opening. Receiving a grant does not mint a token and does not permit a second mint by an account that already minted. Transfers and approvals remain available during the launch window.

Browsing no longer has a fixed 15-minute timeout. Phil still locks on manual lock, Mac lock/sleep or quit. Fresh Mac confirmation is required for each transaction, with a single-use signing grant limited to 30 seconds. This does not delete your saved artwork choice or retry an operation. Unlock again to continue. An unresolved signing attempt pauses new mint and transfer actions until its status is checked.
